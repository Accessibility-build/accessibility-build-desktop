The first release of Accessibility Build for Mac, a free menu-bar app for checking accessibility anywhere on your screen.

**Contrast picker.** Freeze the screen and pick text and background colors with a magnified loupe, by mouse or entirely by keyboard. See WCAG 2.2 results for normal text, large text and UI components, the APCA Lc value as guidance, the nearest passing colors, and the pair as four color-vision deficiencies may see it. Drag across a gradient or photo to use its lowest-contrast pixel.

**Color-vision lens.** A floating lens that shows what is beneath it as protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia or achromatopsia, with severity, blur and contrast-loss filters and a split before and after view.

**Captures.** A region, the front window or a whole display, optionally after a three-second delay for hover states and menus. Copied to the clipboard and savable as PNG.

**Session log.** Every pick and capture, exported as a Markdown summary.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel. Signed with our Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Screen pixels are processed on your Mac and never uploaded; the only network request is the update check. Verify the download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
